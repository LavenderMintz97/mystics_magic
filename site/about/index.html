About

Intuition with structure.

Mystics Magic is a personal reading practice focused on Tarot, Lenormand, and Elder Futhark Rune guidance.

For clients who want meaning without losing the ground.

The reading style is reflective, warm, and direct. Each reading is built to help clients see patterns, receive guidance, and choose the next small action with more calm.

Vanessa focuses on text-only Tarot, Lenormand, and Elder Futhark Rune readings with full English reports, clear boundaries, and respectful privacy. Every offering keeps the client at the center: cards and runes provide language, but your lived experience decides what becomes useful.
